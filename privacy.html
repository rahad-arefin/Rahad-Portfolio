Privacy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This site exists so you can learn about my work. I keep data collection small and never sell or share it for advertising.

What is measured

  • Google Analytics 4 counts visits, pages viewed, where visitors come from, and actions such as downloading my resume or tapping a contact button. Your IP address is not stored by Google Analytics 4.
  • Microsoft Clarity shows anonymised heatmaps and session replays so I can fix confusing parts of the site. Text you type into fields is masked.
  • Both tools are loaded through Google Tag Manager and only run with your consent where the law requires it.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them, like any web request.

What is not collected

No advertising cookies, no ad retargeting, and no forms that store your details on this site. The Growth Health Check and ROAS calculator run entirely in your browser; your answers are not saved.
