My philosophy

Where growth and people come together.

Over the past 8 years I’ve led campaigns and teams across different markets. Marketing is not just about numbers. It’s about understanding what people need and connecting with them in a meaningful way.

I work in a collaborative, structured way. I bring clarity to complex situations and make sure everyone understands the why behind what we’re doing, because that’s when the best work happens.

“Outside of work, I enjoy reading and watching science fiction. It keeps me curious about how systems, people and future trends evolve.”

Coach, don’t micromanage

Business growth requires team growth. Leading 24 people across SEO, PPC, creative and operations means building systems that let people perform consistently without constant supervision. Process-first thinking wins.

Loops beat funnels

Marketing doesn’t fail in isolation. Funnels assume a straight line. I build self-reinforcing loops where acquisition feeds retention and retention funds the next round of growth.

Revenue over vanity metrics

Clicks and cheap leads look good in a report. I judge marketing on what reaches the pipeline: cost per qualified lead, booked meetings and revenue per visitor.

Fix the business, not just the ads

Ads can’t outrun a broken sales process. The client who grew from 1 to 14 businesses did so because we fixed operations, training and reporting alongside the campaigns.

Test with discipline

80% of budget goes to what’s proven and 20% to structured experiments. Growth comes from compounding small, measured wins rather than big bets.

Automate the repeatable

Let AI and automation handle reporting, data entry and first drafts, so people spend their time on strategy, creativity and relationships.
